How to make a great intro video

Length: 60–90 seconds, and no more than 2 minutes.

Shape: film horizontally (landscape, 16:9) so it fits well on phones and computers.

What to say:

  1. Hello, your name and where you're from.
  2. How long you've been teaching and your key qualifications.
  3. Who you love teaching (e.g. beginners, IELTS, business).
  4. What a lesson with you feels like.
  5. One personal touch: a hobby or something you enjoy.
  6. A warm invitation: "I'd love to help you reach your goals."

Filming tips: quiet room · face a window for light · camera at eye level · look at the lens and smile · speak clearly at a natural pace · short Italian subtitles are a bonus.

Sharing it: upload to YouTube as Unlisted (or Vimeo / Loom) and paste the link above.
